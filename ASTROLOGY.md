## 4. Forensic Tools: Lots, Nodes, and Stars

### The Hermetic Lots (Klēroi)
Abstract mathematical points synthesizing Sun, Moon, and Ascendant.
*   **Lot of Fortune (Tyche)**: Body, Chance, Health.
*   **Lot of Spirit (Daimon)**: Soul, Career, Volition.
*   **Forensic Formulas (Strict A + B - C)**:
    *   **Lot of Eros**: *Day*: Asc + Spirit - Fortune | *Night*: Asc + Fortune - Spirit. (Desire for union).
    *   **Lot of Necessity (Anankē)**: *Day*: Asc + Fortune - Spirit | *Night*: Asc + Spirit - Fortune. (Constraint, enemies, taxes).
    *   **Lot of Victory (Nikē)**: Derived from Spirit and Jupiter.
    *   **Parental Lots**: Formulas provided for Father (Sun/Saturn) and Mother (Moon/Venus).

### The Draconic Engine (Lunar Nodes)
Mechanism of **Material Volume**, not spiritual morality.
*   **North Node (Caput Draconis)**: *Intake/Anabolism*. The "Maw." Amplifies and inflates.
    *   *With Benefics*: Massive abundance, potential for gluttony/bloat.
    *   *With Malefics*: Structural giants, massive obstruction.
*   **South Node (Cauda Draconis)**: *Excretion/Catabolism*. The "Sphincter/Drain." Diminishes, spectralizes, creates voids/ghosts.
    *   *With Planets*: Strips them of form. "Starving Mother" (Moon), "Tragic Lover" (Venus).
*   **The Bendings (Square to Nodes)**: Maximum Latitudinal Stress. points of "Wobble" and structural fracture.
    *   *North Bending*: Hyper-exposure/Explosion of themes.
    *   *South Bending*: Implosion/Internal collapse.

*   **The Celestial Curia**:
