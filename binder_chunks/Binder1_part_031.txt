1. Introduction: The Geometric Architecture of Fate  

In the forensic reconstruction of Hellenistic astrology, the "Hermetic Lots" (Klēroi) emerge

projected onto the ecliptic plane from the horizon. They represent the "alchemical" synthesis  
of three distinct loci: two planets (the significators) and the Ascendant (the native’s specific  
terrestrial anchor). This triangulation serves to ground the celestial archetypes into the  
immediate, lived reality of the nativity.  

The investigation into these points requires a departure from modern psychological  
abstraction and a return to the rigorous, forensic methodology of the 1st through 13th  
centuries. Our primary witnesses in this reconstruction are Vettius Valens, whose Anthology  
serves as the foundational text for the practical application of the Lots in the 2nd century;  
Guido Bonatti, whose 13th-century Liber Astronomiae bridges the Arabic and Latin traditions;  
and Abu Rayhan al-Biruni, whose 11th-century Instruction in the Elements of the Art of  
Astrology provides a critical catalog of the proliferation of these points in the medieval era.  

The analysis that follows establishes a definitive technical guide to the calculation,  
jurisdiction, and activation of the critical Hermetic Lots. We aim to strip away later

Special emphasis is placed on the Geometry of Sect—the critical reversal of formulas based  
on Diurnal and Nocturnal genitures—and the chronological activation of these points via the  
Time Lord systems of Annual Profections and Zodiacal Releasing.  

2. The Geometry of Calculation: Arc, Sect, and Projection  

The calculation of a Lot is a geometric operation involving the measurement of an arc  
between two points and the projection of that arc from a third. While modern software often  
reduces these operations to algebraic equations, the forensic mathematician must  
﻿understand the spatial logic dictated by the "Condition of the Heavens" (Sect).  

The general formula is expressed algebraically as:  

  
$$\text{Lot} = \text{Ascendant} + \text{Planet}_B - \text{Planet}_A$$  
However, this algebraic simplicity masks the geometric reality required for accurate  
derivation. The formula instructs the astrologer to measure the zodiacal longitude from  
Planet A (the Initiator) to Planet B (the Terminator) in the order of the signs (counter- 
clockwise) and add that resulting arc distance to the degree of the Ascendant. This  
projection creates a "second Ascendant" specific to the topic of the two planets involved.  

2.1 The Imperative of Sect (Diurnal vs. Nocturnal)  

The most critical deviation in modern practice is the neglect of Sect. The Hellenistic tradition,  
particularly as codified by Valens and Dorotheus of Sidon, posits that the "condition of the  
heavens"—whether the chart is Diurnal (Day) or Nocturnal (Night)—alters the directionality of  
the flow of influence. This is not a trivial nuance but a fundamental operational constraint.  

  ●   Diurnal (Day) Genitures:  Defined when the Sun is above the horizon (the  
      Ascendant/Descendant axis). In this condition, the formulas typically follow the solar or  
      "active" direction, often initiating the arc from the Sect Light (Sun) or the masculine  
      significator.  
  ●   Nocturnal (Night) Genitures:  Defined when the Sun is below the horizon. In this  
      condition, the formulas typically reverse (Planet A and Planet B swap roles) to reflect the  
      lunar or "reactive" geometry. This reversal maintains the symmetrical relationship of the  
      Lot relative to the horizon, a principle emphasized in the Carmen Astrologicum of

3. Forensic Reconstruction of the Critical Lots  

The identification of the correct formulas for the "Hermetic" Lots requires navigating a

(Paulus Alexandrinus). The "Hermetic" sets are generally considered those derived from the  
interplay of the two principal lights and the two principal lots (Fortune and Spirit), whereas  
the "Planetary" lots of Paulus utilize the planets directly. For the purpose of this manual, we  
prioritize the Valens/Hermetic tradition where applicable, as it represents the root logic of the  
﻿system used in Zodiacal Releasing.  

3.1 The Lot of Eros (Erōs)  

Jurisdiction: Relationships, Voluntary Association, Desires of the Soul.  

The Lot of Eros signifies the appetites, voluntative desires, and the drive for union. It is the  
geometric counterpart to the Lot of Necessity. While Paulus Alexandrinus introduces a  
formula using Venus directly (Asc + Venus - Spirit), the archaic method favored by Vettius  
Valens derives Eros from the relationship between the Lot of Fortune ( Tychē) and the Lot of  
Spirit (Daimōn). This identifies Eros as the "desire of the Spirit for the Body" or the drive for  
the union of will and circumstance.4

  ●   Nocturnal (Night) Calculation:  
        
      $$\text{Ascendant} + \text{Lot of Fortune} - \text{Lot of Spirit}$$  
       ○   Geometric Logic: Measure the arc from Spirit to Fortune; project from the  
           Ascendant.  

Analysis:  
In a Day chart, the arc moves from Fortune (Matter) to Spirit (Will). This implies that "Eros" is  
the lifting of the material up to the spiritual/volitional—a desire for higher union or conscious  
selection of partners. The Spirit desires the substance of Fortune. In the Night chart, the  
reversal suggests a grounding of the Spirit into Fortune, perhaps indicating more visceral or  
"fated" attractions where the Will seeks embodiment. It is critical to note that for Zodiacal  
Releasing regarding relationships, this "Lot of Lots" formula (Spirit/Fortune based) is the  
standard technique, rather than the Venus-based formula of Paulus.1

holds the cosmos together, often manifesting in human life as "compulsory" events. It is  
derived from the same points as Eros (Fortune and Spirit) but projected in the opposite  
direction, creating a symmetrical axis of Fate.1

       ○   Forensic Application:  If the Lot of Necessity is in Gemini, and the native enters a

           outcome is determined by the condition of Mercury (the ruler) in the natal chart and

  2.   Activation of the Lot's Ruler:  If the Lord of the Year is the domicile ruler of the Lot, the

       ○   Forensic Application:  If the Lot of Victory is in Pisces, ruled by Jupiter, and the

5.2 Activation via Zodiacal Releasing (Aphesis)  

Zodiacal Releasing is the most sophisticated timing technique for the Lots, specifically  
designed to "release" the time debt of the Lots of Spirit and Fortune. However, Valens and  
the Hermetic authors imply the technique can be applied to other Lots to time their specific  
topics.  

  ●   The Peak Periods: The Lot is activated most intensely when the current Time Lord sign  
      is angular (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) to the position of the Lot itself.  
       ○   Forensic Application: When releasing from the Lot of Spirit (Career/Action), if the  
           period reaches the sign of the Lot of Victory, this indicates a "Peak" or culminating  
           period for enterprise. It often coincides with promotions, successful contests, or the

           Necessity during a Spirit release may indicate a career period defined by struggle or  
           "holding patterns".16  

 ●    Loosing of the Bond (LB): This phenomenon occurs when the Releasing sequence  
      jumps from a sign of long ascension to one of short ascension (e.g., Pisces to  
      Libra/Scorpio in specific sub-periods).  
       ○   Forensic Application:  If the "Loosing of the Bond" occurs while the Lot of Necessity  
           is activated, it signifies a chaotic but potentially liberating breaking of chains—a  
           sudden release from prison, the unexpected end of a debt, or the collapse of a  
           restrictive structure. It is a moment where the "inevitable" is disrupted by a shift in  
           the cosmic mechanism.17  

6. The Lot of Necessity: A Forensic Deep Dive  

The Lot of Necessity (Anankē) is the specific jurisdiction of "unavoidable fate." While the Lot  
of Fortune represents the body and chance events that befall us, Necessity represents the  
constraints that bind us. It is the "Hard Fate" distinct from the "Soft Fate" of Fortune. In the  
forensic analysis of a nativity, Necessity is the primary diagnostic tool for identifying the  
source of enmity and suffering.  

6.1 Identifying the Source of Unavoidable Suffering  

To diagnose the specific nature of a native's burdens, the astrologer must analyze the  
"Celestial State" of the Lot of Necessity using three vectors:  

  1.   The Ruler of the Lot (The Agent): The planet ruling the sign of Necessity identifies who  
      or what enforces the constraint.

7. Conclusion  

The "Hermetic Lots" are not arbitrary points but a sophisticated geometric language used to  
calculate the specific vectors of destiny. By adhering to the forensic protocols of Sect  
(Diurnal/Nocturnal reversal) and calculating the arcs with precision, the astrologer unlocks a  
layer of the chart that speaks to the "inevitable" (Necessity), the "desirous" (Eros), and the  
"triumphant" (Victory).  

The activation of these points via Profections and Zodiacal Releasing provides the temporal  
"when" to the thematic "what," allowing the forensic mathematician to predict not just the  
nature of an event, but its arrival. The Lot of Necessity, in particular, stands as the sentinel of  
the native's burdens, revealing that suffering is rarely random, but rather a calculated  
variable in the geometry of the soul's incarnation.
