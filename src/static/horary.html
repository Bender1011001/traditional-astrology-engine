What Is Horary Astrology?

The oldest question-answering technique in Western astrology.

Horary astrology is a branch of traditional astrology that answers a specific question by erecting a chart for the exact moment the question is sincerely asked. Unlike natal astrology — which maps your entire life from birth — horary focuses on one question, one moment, one answer.

The method was perfected by William Lilly in his 1647 masterwork Christian Astrology, though its roots trace back to the Arabic astrologers of the 8th–10th centuries (Masha'allah, Sahl ibn Bishr) and ultimately to the Hellenistic tradition. It remains the most empirically testable branch of astrology — you ask, the chart answers, and life confirms or denies.

Key Horary Principles

The classical rules that govern every judgment.

✦ Significators

The Lord of the Ascendant represents you. The Lord of the relevant house represents what you're asking about. Aspects between them — or not — determine the outcome.

✦ Perfection

If the significators are applying to a major aspect (conjunction, sextile, trine, square, opposition), the matter "perfects" — meaning the answer tends toward Yes. No applying aspect means No.

✦ Translation of Light

A faster planet can carry the light from one significator to the other, acting as a go-between or intermediary. This is a classical mechanism for things coming together through a third party.

✦ Prohibition

When a third planet aspects one of the significators before they can perfect, it "prohibits" — blocks — the outcome. Something or someone interferes.

✦ Strictures Against Judgment

Certain chart conditions — early Ascendant, late Ascendant, Saturn in the 7th, void-of-course Moon — indicate the chart may not be fit for judgment. The engine checks and reports these transparently.

✦ Regiomontanus Houses

Classical horary uses Regiomontanus house division, not whole sign. This was the standard for Lilly and the Renaissance horary tradition. House cusps are calculated from real astronomical data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is horary astrology?

Horary astrology is a branch of traditional astrology that answers a specific question by erecting a chart for the exact moment the question is asked. It uses strict classical rules from William Lilly, Bonatti, and the Arabic tradition to deliver a concrete answer — not a personality reading, but a direct judgment about your question.

Do I need my birth time for horary?

No. Horary has nothing to do with your natal chart. The chart is erected for the moment you ask the question. You only need your current city so the engine can calculate precise house cusps and planetary positions for your location right now.

What are strictures against judgment?

Strictures are chart conditions that indicate the question may not be answerable at this time — for example, if the Ascendant is in the first or last 3° of a sign, if Saturn afflicts the 7th house, or if the Moon is void of course. When strictures appear, the engine reports them honestly. The answer may still be given, but with a warning.
