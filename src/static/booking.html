How Booking Works

  1. Complete the Chart Audit: Start with the audit to validate data and determine scope.
  2. Submit a Scope Request: After the audit, send your objective and requested service. We confirm whether traditional doctrine supports it.
  3. Reserve Your Slot: Approved clients receive a payment link and scheduling window. Payment confirms the booking.

Availability

We accept a limited number of clients each month to ensure accuracy and depth. If the calendar is full, we maintain a waitlist and prioritize those who have completed an audit.

Contact for Scope Approval

After completing your audit, send a concise request with your objectives. Include the audit ID and any time-sensitive constraints.
