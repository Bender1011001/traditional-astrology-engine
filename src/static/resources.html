Traditional Astrology Defined

Traditional astrology is the pre-modern discipline practiced from the Hellenistic era through the Renaissance. It relies on the septenary planets, sect, essential dignities, and established delineation rules. Its goal is judgment of concrete conditions, not personality counseling.

Codex Caelestis preserves this logic by prioritizing deterministic technique and named sources. Statements are rule-derived and auditable.

Essential Dignity & Planetary Condition

The doctrine of essential dignities is the engine of traditional judgment. It measures a planet's capacity to act within a chart and assigns weighted scores based on rulership, exaltation, triplicity, term, and face.

High dignity indicates authority and effectiveness. Debility indicates constraint and reliance on external support.

Timing Techniques in Context

Timing is not prediction by intuition; it is the disciplined use of profections, time lords, and period rulers. These techniques organize life periods and tell us which planetary significations are activated in a given year.

Codex Caelestis treats timing as a structured ledger so clients can evaluate outcomes against defined rules.
