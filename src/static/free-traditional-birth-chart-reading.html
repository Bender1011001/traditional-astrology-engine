Why a traditional astrology chart is different

Traditional astrology gives planets condition and responsibility. A planet is not only in a sign; it rules houses, gains or loses dignity, belongs to a day or night chart, and interacts with other planets by aspect and reception. That is why this page targets traditional astrology chart and traditional birth chart searches directly.
