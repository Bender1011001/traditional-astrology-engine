What the free PDF preview includes

The free chart preview is built from the same calculation pipeline as the paid reading. It gives you a compact, printable view of the traditional chart conditions that matter first.

Day or night chart

Sect is calculated from the Sun's position relative to the horizon, so benefics and malefics are judged in the correct day/night context.

Planets and houses

Classical planets are placed by sign and house using Whole Sign houses, with the chart ruler and angular emphasis surfaced clearly.

Timing preview

The reading includes annual profection context so the current year is not treated as a static natal description.

  1. Open the free chart form and enter your birth date, time, city, and state or country.
