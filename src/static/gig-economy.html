THE PROBLEM EVERY ETSY ASTROLOGER HAS

You're spending hours on work the engine can do in seconds.

Order comes in

A buyer sends their birth date, time, and place through Etsy. You manually copy it somewhere.

You calculate by hand

You open your software, enter the data, run the chart, export whatever you can, then rebuild the report in a template.

You format it

Copy-paste into Google Docs or Canva, fix the spacing, add the chart wheel, export as PDF. For every single order.

You deliver it

Upload to Etsy, email the buyer, handle revisions. Then do it all over again for the next order.

If you have 10 orders a week, that's 10–20 hours of manual work. Traditional Astrology turns that into 10 minutes.

THE Traditional Astrology WORKFLOW

Three steps. Works with any order volume.

Step 1: Export Your Etsy Orders

Download your orders from Etsy as a CSV (or build one from your intake form). Traditional Astrology accepts standard headers (name, date, time, city, state) and Etsy-style aliases (client_name, birth_date, birth_time, birth_place).

Step 2: Upload the CSV

In your Traditional Astrology dashboard, drop the CSV into the Batch Tool. The engine validates each row and shows you a preview before generating. Missing birth times? It defaults to noon and keeps going.

Step 3: Download the ZIP

One click. You get a single ZIP file with one professional PDF per client row. Rename and deliver — or let Traditional Astrology name them automatically by client name.
