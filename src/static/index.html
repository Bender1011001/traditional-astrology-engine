What's In a Reading

Not a personality quiz. Here's what you actually get.

✦ Planetary Positions

All seven classical planets — Sun through Saturn — placed by sign, degree, and house. We use the traditional rulers, not Uranus/Neptune/Pluto.

✦ Sect & Temperament

Day birth or night birth? It sounds simple, but sect changes which planets work for you and which ones cause trouble. Most modern sites ignore this entirely.

✦ Essential Dignities

Every planet gets a dignity score — domicile, exaltation, triplicity, term, face. A planet in its own sign operates completely differently from one in detriment. The scores show you who's strong in your chart.

✦ Current Life Period

Profections tell you which house is activated this year and which planet is running the show. Firdaria maps the longer chapters. Together they explain why certain years feel completely different from others.

✦ Lot of Fortune

Calculated from Sun, Moon, and Ascendant — this ancient point describes your material circumstances and physical constitution. People are usually surprised by how specific it is.

✦ Fixed Stars

Regulus, Spica, Algol — if a bright fixed star falls near one of your key chart points, it adds a layer that planets alone don't explain. Think of it as the wild card.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is traditional astrology?

The astrology that existed before about 1700 — Greek, Persian, medieval, Renaissance. It's more concrete than modern astrology. Instead of "you're a creative soul," traditional astrology says things like "your 10th house ruler is debilitated in the 12th, so career visibility comes late and through institutions." Specific, testable, often uncomfortably accurate.

Do I need to know my birth time?

Ideally, yes — your rising sign, houses, and all the timing techniques depend on it. If you don't have it, we'll still calculate planet positions and dignities, but we'll skip the parts that would be unreliable without a time. The report is upfront about what's missing.

How is this different from free astrology sites?

Most free sites tell you your sun is in Gemini and leave it at that. We compute the full chart — all seven classical planets with dignity scores, sect analysis, Arabic lots, and a timing forecast using profections and firdaria. And we show the math, so you can verify any claim against the raw data.

Is this AI-generated?

The chart itself is pure math — astronomical positions from the Swiss Ephemeris, run through deterministic rules. Same birth data, same chart, every time. The narrative text uses AI to turn those numbers into readable English, but every statement in the reading traces back to a specific calculation. We're not making things up.

Do I need an account?

No. The free reading requires no account and no email. The complete 20+ page analysis is a one-time purchase through Stripe — still no account needed, and the PDF is emailed to you. If anything goes wrong with delivery, you keep the reading and get your money back.
