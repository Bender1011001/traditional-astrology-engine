Pillars of Traditional Practice

Classical Sources

We privilege primary texts and traditional transmission: Greek, Persian, Medieval, and Renaissance. No outer-planet reinterpretations, no New Age overlays.

Deterministic Technique

Judgment follows rules: essential dignities, sect, house rulership, angularity, and time lords. Technique precedes narrative.

Forensic Method

We treat a chart like a case file: evidence is weighted, contradictions are named, and uncertainty is declared.

Services
