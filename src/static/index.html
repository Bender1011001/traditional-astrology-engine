Frequently Asked Questions

What is traditional astrology?

Traditional astrology refers to the methods used before the 1700s — the original techniques from ancient Greece, Persia, and medieval Europe. It's more predictive and specific than modern psychological astrology. We use techniques from Vettius Valens, Guido Bonatti, and Dorotheus of Sidon.

Do I need to know my birth time?

For the most accurate reading, yes. Your birth time determines your rising sign, house placements, and timing techniques. If you don't know your time, we can still calculate your planetary positions, but the reading will be less detailed.

How is this different from free astrology sites?

Most free sites give you a paragraph about your sun sign. We calculate your complete natal chart using real ephemeris data and interpret it using classical techniques — dignities, lots, profections, firdaria — the same methods professional traditional astrologers use. Every claim is auditable against the calculation data.

Is this AI-generated?

The calculations are 100% deterministic — computed from real astronomical data, not generated by AI. The narrative interpretation uses AI to translate the technical data into readable language, but every fact in the reading comes from the verified calculation engine. Same birth data always produces the same chart.

Do I need an account?

No. Enter your birth details, get your reading. If you want the full PDF, you pay once through Stripe — no account, no password, no email required. We don't store your personal data.

Can I get a refund?

Digital goods are non-refundable, but if your reading has a genuine technical error (wrong calculations, missing data), contact us and we'll make it right.
