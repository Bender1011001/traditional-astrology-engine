What is the Almuten Figuris?

In traditional astrology, the Almuten Figuris (or "Ruler of the Chart") is not simply your Rising Sign or Sun Sign. It is the single planet in your chart that holds the most power and authority over your life's direction.

Derived from the Latin Almuten ("The Winner/The Victor"), this planet acts as the Captain of the Soul. While your Sun represents your ego drive and your Moon represents your physical body, the Almuten Figuris represents the spiritual entity guiding the ship.

How It Is Calculated

We use the method of the 12th-century scholar Abraham Ibn Ezra. This is a rigorous comprehensive analysis of the chart that scores planets based on:

  • Essential Dignity: Rulership, Exaltation, Triplicity, Term, and Face.
  • House Position: Planets in angular houses (1st, 10th, 7th, 4th) gain massive power.
  • Day vs. Night: The "Sect" of the chart determines which planetary rulers are active.
Why it matters: Knowing your Almuten gives you a "North Star." It tells you *how* you best succeed. A Mars Almuten succeeds through conflict and friction. A Venus Almuten succeeds through unification and art.
