Purpose

Traditional-Astrology.com exists to restore the discipline of classical astrology: the rule-based interpretation of natal promise, timing, and concrete circumstance. We do not modernize the art, dilute its rigor, or recast it as psychological coaching.

Lineage & Sources

Our practice rests on primary sources from the Hellenistic, Persian, Medieval, and Renaissance traditions. We use the seven visible planets, the doctrine of sect, essential dignities, whole sign houses, time lords, and validated predictive methods.

  • Hellenistic: Dorotheus, Valens, Ptolemy, Antiochus.
  • Persian & Arabic: Masha'allah, Abu Ma'shar, al-Qabisi.
  • Medieval & Renaissance: Bonatti, Lilly, Morin (where doctrinally aligned).

Method

Every judgment begins with a technical audit. We establish chart reliability, the condition of luminaries, essential dignities, and house lords. Only then do we interpret. If data is insufficient, we state limitations explicitly.

Boundaries

  • No therapy: We do not provide mental health services or counseling.
  • No vague spirituality: Interpretations are grounded in doctrine, not intuition.
  • No deterministic guarantees: We state the likely range of outcomes and the evidential basis.
  • No medical advice: Historical medical techniques are discussed only as historical context.

Client Fit

This work is for clients who value clarity, structure, and accountability. We are selective about engagements to preserve quality. If your request cannot be served by traditional doctrine, we will decline it.
