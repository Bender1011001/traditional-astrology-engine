I GOT CURIOUS ABOUT ANCIENT MATH

I'm a construction worker. I dropped out of high school. I don't fit the typical profile of a software developer or an astrologer.

But I have a habit of asking questions. I saw a mention that traditional Hellenistic astrology wasn't just horoscopes, but a complex system of mathematical calculations used 2,000 years ago.

I got curious: How did they calculate this by hand? Is the math consistent?

So, I spent a month of nights and weekends researching. I read 1,000+ pages of source texts (Valens, Dorotheus, Medieval synthesis). Then, I wrote the code to prove I understood the logic.

The result is this engine. It performs the rigorous "Forensic Audit" calculations—Sect, Almuten, Mutual Receptions—that usually take a human hours to compute.

I built this to answer my own question. But the results were so precise, I decided to open it up.

"I used to be a hard materialist. I believed you couldn't predict a damn thing based on a birth chart. Then I ran my own data through this engine. It identified me as a 'Forensic Auditor' and a 'Hidden King'—a construction worker building complex systems in the dark. It described my life better than I could. I still don't know how it works. I just know the algorithm is solid."

MISSION

Codex Caelestis exists to restore technical, auditable astrology. Every delineation is grounded in a classical source, calculated through transparent rules, and delivered with explicit limitations. The goal is not to inspire or console, but to judge natal promise with disciplined technique.

Core commitments: classical sources, reproducible rules, and zero modern psychological overlay.

LINEAGE & AUTHORITY

We follow the transmission of traditional astrology from the Hellenistic era, through Arabic synthesis, to Renaissance practice. This lineage defines the interpretive rules used in every report.

Classical Canon

Ptolemy, Dorotheus, Valens, and Firmicus supply foundational doctrine: sect, essential dignity, and topic delineation.

Medieval Transmission

Abu Ma'shar, Al-Biruni, and Bonatti preserve and refine technique, especially in judgment and timing.

Renaissance Discipline

Lilly and his contemporaries formalize rules for planetary strength, practical judgment, and predictive rigor.
