MISSION STATEMENT

Codex Caelestis was built to bridge the gap between rigorous traditional astrology and modern accessibility. Most astrology today is either overly generalized "pop" astrology or dense, inaccessible academic texts.

Our mission is to provide forensic, rule-based readings that honor the complexity of the ancient tradition without overwhelming the user. We believe astrology should be clear, specific, and rooted in an audit trail of historical techniques.

THE CREATOR

This engine was developed by a practitioner dedicated to the revival of Hellenistic and Medieval astrological techniques. Frustrated by the subjectivity of modern psychological astrology, I sought to build a system that acts more like a "cosmic clock" than a therapy session.

My background combines software engineering with years of study in primary source texts—from Vettius Valens to William Lilly. This duality allows distinct translation of ancient rules into deterministic code.

Why "Forensic"?
Because the results should stand up to scrutiny. Every statement in your reading comes from a specific line of code that represents a specific sentence in a classical text.

WHAT MAKES THIS UNIQUE
