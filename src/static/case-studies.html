Case Studies

The best way to test a technique is to run it on a chart where you already know what happened. These are four famous charts analyzed with traditional methods — profections, firdaria, zodiacal releasing, dignities — to see whether the techniques actually match the biography.

Zodiacal Releasing

Steve Jobs

The Launch of the iPhone (2007)

Birth Data: Feb 24, 1955, 19:15, San Francisco, CA

Zodiacal Releasing from the Lot of Spirit tracks career peaks and lifetime prominence. Using the Egyptian bounds and Hellenistic time lords, we calculated Jobs' periods.

The Forensic Evidence

In January 2007, when Jobs announced the first iPhone, his Level 1 Spirit period shifted into an angular sign (Aries). Simultaneously, his Level 2 lord reached the "Loosing of the Bond," an ancient indicator of a massive career leap and an unprecedented structural shift in one’s legacy.

What This Shows

Zodiacal Releasing identified the most significant career shift of Jobs' life within the correct year. The technique doesn't know about smartphones — it tracks when the chart's career indicators peak. The timing lined up.

Techniques used: Lot of Spirit, Zodiacal Releasing, Sect.
Essential Dignities

Queen Elizabeth II

Ascension to the Throne (1952)

Birth Data: April 21, 1926, 02:40, London, UK

A monarch's chart relies heavily on the strength of the Sun, the Ascendant lord, and the Midheaven (10th house of public standing).

The Forensic Evidence

Born at night (Nocturnal Sect), her chart features a hugely dignified Jupiter in Aquarius angular to her Midheaven, but the striking feature is Saturn, Lord of the Ascendant, placed exactly on the Midheaven. Saturn represents duty, time, and heavy burdens. Furthermore, at her ascension in 1952, her Annual Profection reached the 10th House, activating this exact Saturn.

What This Shows

Saturn on the Midheaven tells you the career involves heavy, lifelong structural responsibility. The profection to the 10th house in 1952 activated that exact Saturn. The chart described the role before it happened — duty, public burden, permanence.

Techniques used: Annual Profections, Essential Dignities, Midheaven Lords.
Firdaria & Lots

J.R.R. Tolkien

Publishing The Lord of the Rings (1954)

Birth Data: Jan 3, 1892, 20:00, Bloemfontein, SA

Firdaria is a Persian timing technique dividing life into planetary periods. We examine Tolkien's periods during the release of his masterwork.

The Forensic Evidence

Tolkien published The Fellowship of the Ring in 1954. At this time, his Firdaria shifted into the sub-period of Venus, while his major period was Mercury. Mercury rules writing, language, and translation (he was a philologist). Venus, acting as a highly dignified benefic in his chart related to his 9th house of publishing and higher knowledge, provided the precise timing trigger for his manuscript's release.

What This Shows

A Mercury major period activating during the publication of a lifelong writing project, with Venus (benefic, 9th-house publishing) as the sub-period lord. The firdaria didn't cause the book — Tolkien wrote it over decades — but it timed when the private work became public.

Techniques used: Persian Firdaria, Derived Houses, Benefic Activations.
Hyleg / Alcocoden

Marilyn Monroe

The Tragedy of the 12th House

Birth Data: June 1, 1926, 09:30, Los Angeles, CA

Traditional astrology doesn't romanticize difficult placements. We calculate the Hyleg (giver of life) and Alcocoden (giver of years) to assess vitality.

The Forensic Evidence

Monroe's Ascendant lord (Sun) and her Moon were severely afflicted. More importantly, her Lot of Fortune was heavily dominated by a malefic Saturn in the 12th House (the house of self-undoing, isolation, and mental anguish). When we calculate her primary directions hitting this 12th house Saturn, it perfectly aligned with her tragic final year in 1962.

What This Shows

A compromised Hyleg and a Lot of Fortune dominated by Saturn in the 12th house describe a life where material fortune is entangled with isolation and hidden suffering. The primary directions to that Saturn timed the year. Traditional techniques don't romanticize difficult charts — they describe them plainly.

Techniques used: Hyleg & Alcocoden, 12th House Afflictions, Primary Directions.
