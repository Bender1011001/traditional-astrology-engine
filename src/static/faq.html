What if I don't know my birth time?

If the birth time is unknown, we can still calculate planetary positions by sign, but we cannot reliably judge houses, Ascendant/MC, or time-sensitive techniques. We state those limits clearly and avoid speculative interpretation.

How is this different from other astrology sites?

We use traditional, rule-based judgment. The focus is on natal promise, concrete circumstances, and timing—derived from essential dignities, sect, house rulership, and time lords. We do not use modern psychological archetypes or outer-planet symbolism.

What does \"traditional\" mean?

Traditional astrology refers to the doctrines practiced from the 1st century BCE through the Renaissance. It emphasizes essential dignity, sect, and established predictive techniques. We work primarily with the seven visible planets and whole sign houses.
