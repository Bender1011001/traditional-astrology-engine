What if I don't know my birth time?

If you don't know your exact birth time, you can select "Time unknown" in the form. We will use a default time (typically noon) to calculate planetary positions. However, please note that the Ascendant, Midheaven, Houses, and certain time-sensitive Lots (like the Lot of Fortune) cannot be accurately calculated without a birth time. Your reading will focus on the planets in signs and aspects, which remain valid regardless of time.

What does "traditional" mean?

"Traditional" refers to the astrological doctrines practiced from approximately 1st century BCE to the 17th century. It emphasizes Essential Dignity (planetary strength), Sect (Day/Night charts), and concrete prediction over psychological analysis. We use the 7 visible planets (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon) as the primary actors.
