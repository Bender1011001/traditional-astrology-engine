What makes this traditional rather than modern?

We adhere to the septenary planets, essential dignity scoring, sect doctrine, and classical house logic. We do not use modern outer planets as primary rulers, psychological framing, or vague affirmations. Our sources are cited and traceable.

What if I do not know my birth time?

If your birth time is unknown, we will generate a limited judgment based on planets in signs and aspects. We will omit houses, angles, lots, and timing techniques that require an accurate time. This limitation is clearly stated in the report.

Do you offer counseling or spiritual guidance?

No. We provide astrological judgments using traditional technique. We do not provide mental health counseling, spiritual coaching, or replacement for professional advice.

Why is paid access required?

Full dossiers require intensive calculation and archival storage. Paid access funds the ongoing research, source maintenance, and computation needed to keep the system rigorous. The preliminary judgment is available to verify fit before purchase.

How quickly will I receive my report?

Reports are generated instantly after submission. Paid access unlocks the full dossier immediately upon confirmed payment.

Is my data private?

Yes. Birth data is used only to generate your report. Payment is processed securely by Stripe, and we do not store card details.

What is the refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for paid access if the report does not meet the stated methodological standard.
