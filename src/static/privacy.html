1. Introduction

Welcome to Codex Caelestis via traditional-astrology.com. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Birth Data: Date, time, and location of birth. This is used solely to calculate your astrological chart.
  • Contact Info: Email address (only if you subscribe or request a PDF).
  • Usage Data: Anonymized logs of site access and errors to improve stability.
  • Payment Data: We do not store credit card numbers. All payments are processed by our secure third-party provider (Stripe).

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your birth data to generate the reading you requested. We use your email to deliver that reading or account-related notifications. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is processed via secure (HTTPS) connections. Birth data for one-time readings is not permanently stored after the session, unless you create an account to save it.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us at support@traditional-astrology.com.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session state (e.g., keeping you logged in). We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.
