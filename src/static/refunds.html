Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 6, 2026

1. One-Time Reading Purchases

Individual readings (Full Reading at $49 and Complete Analysis at $69) are digital products delivered immediately upon payment. Because the reading is generated and delivered instantly, we generally do not offer refunds for completed purchases.

Exception — technical errors: If your reading contains a genuine technical error (e.g., incorrect planetary positions, wrong chart data despite correct input, or a corrupted PDF), contact us within 14 days of purchase at support@traditional-astrology.com with your order details. We will either regenerate your reading or issue a full refund — your choice.

2. Subscription Plans (Scholar & Practitioner)

Monthly subscriptions may be canceled at any time from your dashboard. Cancellation stops future billing, and your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No pro-rated refunds are issued for partial months.

First-month guarantee: If you subscribe and are unsatisfied within the first 7 days of your first paid month, contact support@traditional-astrology.com for a full refund of that month's charge.

3. Free Trials

Free trials do not require a credit card and incur no charge. If you choose to subscribe after a trial, billing begins according to the plan terms displayed at checkout. You will not be charged automatically when a trial ends.

4. Free Chart Previews

The free chart preview (available to all visitors, no account required) has no associated charge and therefore no refund applies.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email support@traditional-astrology.com with your account email and a brief description of the issue. We aim to respond within 1 business day. Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Stripe and typically appear within 5–10 business days.

6. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is unauthorized, please contact us first. We resolve most disputes faster than the chargeback process and can issue refunds directly. Filing a chargeback without contacting us first may result in account suspension while the dispute is investigated.
