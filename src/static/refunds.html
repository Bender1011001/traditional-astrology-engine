14-day technical remedy window: If your reading has a genuine technical or delivery/access problem (for example, incorrect planetary positions despite correct input, wrong chart data, missing paid sections, a corrupted PDF/browser export, or a failed return page after Stripe checkout), contact us within 14 days of purchase at support@traditional-astrology.com with your order details. The free chart preview (available to all visitors, no account required) has no associated charge and therefore no refund applies.

Email support@traditional-astrology.com with your checkout email, order ID if available, birth data used for the report, and a brief description of the issue. We aim to respond within 1 business day. Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Stripe and typically appear within 5–10 business days.

5. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is unauthorized, please contact us first. We resolve most disputes faster than the chargeback process and can issue refunds directly.
