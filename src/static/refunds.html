Refund Policy

Last Updated: February 6, 2026

1. Subscriptions

Subscriptions may be canceled at any time by turning off auto-renewal in your dashboard. Cancellation stops future billing, but access continues until the end of your current billing period.

2. Refunds

We do not provide refunds for the current billing period once a charge has been processed. If you encounter a technical failure that prevents use of the service, please contact support@traditional-astrology.com and we will investigate and apply an account credit when appropriate.

3. Trial

Trials are free and may be started without a credit card. If you subscribe during a trial, billing begins at the end of the trial period.
