# Premium Reading Layer Inventory

This inventory defines the customer-report boundary for the source-first premium
nativity. “Hold” means the engine may calculate the layer, but the report must
not publish it until its formula, provenance, and delineation rules have been
audited. A historical topic is not excluded merely because it touches the sixth
or eighth place; the exclusion applies to direct high-stakes advice and literal
medical, legal, financial, emergency, or longevity claims.

## Never publish as automated direction

- Diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, medication, or surgical timing.
- Investment, debt, estate, contract, lawsuit, or other professional directives.
- Emergency or physical-safety instructions.
- Length-of-life, death-window, hyleg/alcocoden lifespan claims.
- Prescriptive magical remediation represented as necessary or reliably causal.
- Guaranteed events, fate decrees, or fear-based “override” claims.
- Uranus, Neptune, or Pluto as part of the declared pre-modern core.

The underlying historical material may be discussed in scholarship or an
explicitly reviewed research appendix. It does not become automated customer
advice merely because a calculation exists.
