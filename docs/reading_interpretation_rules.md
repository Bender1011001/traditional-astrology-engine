# Traditional Reading Interpretation Rules

Generated: 2026-06-12

This guide consolidates the project documents and structured rule data into an operational "what means what" reference for traditional readings. It is for historical and symbolic interpretation only. Do not present medical, financial, legal, or safety advice as actionable instruction.

## Reading Stack

Use this order when interpreting a reading:

1. Establish chart validity, safety limits, and context.
2. Determine sect: day or night.
3. Read the core planet, sign, house, dignity, and aspect conditions.
4. Identify chart rulers and dominant authorities: Ascendant ruler, Lord of the Year, Almuten Figuris, and relevant time lords.
5. Apply timing systems only after the natal promise is understood.
6. Translate into plain language without removing the rule evidence.

Core formula:

```text
Planet = actor or function
Sign = style and condition
House = life topic
Dignity = ability to act
Aspect = relationship or event mechanics
Sect = whether the planet is operating in its proper environment
Time lord = when the topic is activated
```

## Non-Negotiable Interpretation Constraints

- Use the seven visible planets as the core system: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon.
- Whole Sign houses are the main topical framework.
- Sect always matters. Day and night variants cannot be used interchangeably.
- Outer planets may be secondary modifiers only; they do not replace traditional rulerships.
- The Auditor and Rule Ledger are the authoritative architecture for rule tracing.
- Every judgment should be traceable to a rule, score, source, or structured data table.
- Medical and decumbiture material must be framed as historical symbolism, not health advice.

## Sect

Sect is the first condition gate.

| Condition | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Day chart | Sun is above the horizon. Sun, Jupiter, and Saturn are in sect. |
| Night chart | Sun is below the horizon. Moon, Venus, and Mars are in sect. |
| In sect | Planet operates more coherently and with better environmental support. |
| Out of sect | Planet is more strained, extreme, or less able to express cleanly. |

Interpretive rules:

- Day charts emphasize solar visibility, rank, public action, Jupiterian order, and Saturnian structure.
- Night charts emphasize lunar embodiment, responsiveness, Venusian cohesion, and moderated Mars.
- The malefic contrary to sect is usually harsher: Mars is more difficult by day, Saturn is more difficult by night.
- Hermetic Lot formulas reverse by sect.
- Triplicity rulers are selected by sect.
- Firdaria sequence begins with the Sun by day and the Moon by night.

House strength:

- Angular houses 1, 4, 7, 10: strongest, visible, event-producing.
- Succedent houses 2, 5, 8, 11: sustaining, resource-bearing, slower.
- Cadent houses 3, 6, 9, 12: weaker, mobile, preparatory, or hidden.
- Blind houses 2, 6, 8, 12: do not aspect the Ascendant and often show indirect forces outside conscious control.

## Aspects

| Aspect | Planetary nature | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conjunction | Fusion | Two planets combine or compete in the same place. Judge by dignity, speed, sect, and condition. |
| Sextile | Venus | Cooperation, opportunity, agreement, moderate ease. |
| Square | Mars | Friction, conflict, action, heat, effort, crisis. |
| Trine | Jupiter | Flow, support, protection, easy transmission. |
| Opposition | Saturn | Separation, confrontation, objectification, polarity. |

Aspect mechanics:

- Applying aspects show something forming or moving toward perfection.
- Separating aspects show something already happened, weakening, or leaving.
- Reception determines whether one planet can host or help another.
- Mutual reception can make a difficult matter possible through effort.
- Hard aspects without reception are usually conflict without support.
- Benefic aspects from Venus or Jupiter mitigate.
- Malefic aspects from Mars or Saturn intensify difficulty, especially when the malefic is contrary to sect.

Use dignity to answer: "Can this planet do what it signifies?"

## Temperament

Temperament is the natal baseline. It describes symbolic body style, reactivity, and processing pattern.

Inputs:

- Ascendant sign and Ascendant ruler.
- Planets in the first house and planets aspecting the Ascendant.
- Moon sign, Moon phase, and planets aspecting the Moon.
- Season of birth.
- Almuten of the Figure.

| Quality | Temperament | Symbolic meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot and wet | Sanguine | Growth, circulation, vitality, social ease. |
| Hot and dry | Choleric | Heat, action, speed, irritation, command. |
| Cold and dry | Melancholic | Structure, retention, concentration, heaviness. |
| Cold and wet | Phlegmatic | Cooling, preservation, receptivity, slowness. |

Moon and season mapping:

- New Moon to first quarter: hot/wet, sanguine.
- First quarter to full: hot/dry, choleric.
- Full to last quarter: cold/dry, melancholic.
- Last quarter to new: cold/wet, phlegmatic.
- Spring: hot/wet.
- Summer: hot/dry.
- Autumn: cold/dry.
- Winter: cold/wet.

## Almuten Figuris

Almuten Figuris is the winner of the chart: the planet with the strongest authority over the life pattern.

Core points:

- Sun.
- Moon.
- Ascendant.
- Midheaven or Lot of Fortune depending on the implementation path.
- Prenatal syzygy.

Rules:

- Score the seven traditional planets at each point by essential dignity.
- Add accidental modifiers for house strength, sect luminary, and hayz.
- Highest total wins.
- Tie breakers: highest essential dignity, then highest accidental score, then shortest distance to Ascendant.

Interpretation:

- The winning planet describes the chart's governing style, recurring life-management problem, and dominant capacity.
- Its house shows where authority is concentrated.
- Its condition shows whether that authority is stable, compromised, hidden, or excessive.

## Hyleg And Alcocoden

Historical-use-only vitality technique.

Hyleg:

- Principal life indicator or symbolic giver of life.
- Day selection order: Sun, Moon, Ascendant, Part of Fortune, prenatal lunation.
- Night selection order: Moon, Sun, Ascendant, Part of Fortune, prenatal lunation.
- Must be in a hylegical house: 1, 7, 9, 10, or 11.
- Must be supported by a dignity ruler and not severely afflicted.

Alcocoden:

- Planet that rules the Hyleg's position by dignity and aspects the Hyleg.
- Highest dignity points wins.
- House condition determines whether major, mean, or minor years are symbolically used.

Do not use this as a literal death prediction.

## Hermetic Lots

Lots are calculated points that combine Ascendant, Sun, Moon, and planets. They are not planets; they identify topics and timing anchors.

| Lot | Associated planet | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fortune | Moon | Body, chance, material circumstances, things that happen to the native. |
| Spirit | Sun | Intention, career, action, volition, choices the native makes. |
| Eros | Venus | Desire, attraction, union, friendship, relational longing. |
| Necessity | Mercury | Constraint, compulsion, enemies, burdens, taxes, binding circumstances. |
| Courage | Mars | Boldness, risk, endurance, conflict capacity. |
| Victory | Jupiter | Triumph, legal or public success, breakthrough, aid. |
| Nemesis | Saturn | Downfall, obstruction, consequences, reversals. |

Rules:

- Reverse formulas by sect.
- Choose the Valens/Egyptian or Paulus planetary framework deliberately because downstream timing changes.
- In Zodiacal Releasing, Fortune emphasizes body/circumstance and Spirit emphasizes action/career.

## Prediction Hierarchy

Use this hierarchy:

1. Natal promise: what the chart can actually produce.
2. Firdaria or other long-range time lord: life chapter.
3. Annual profection: annual house and Lord of the Year.
4. Solar return: yearly condition, subordinate to natal promise.
5. Transits: triggers, not root causes.

Rule:

- Do not make transits carry predictions that are not activated by natal promise and time-lord context.

## Annual Profections

Rules:

- Move one Whole Sign house per year from the Ascendant.
- Age 0 activates the 1st house, age 1 the 2nd, and so on in 12-year cycles.
- The ruler of the profected sign is Lord of the Year.
- The activated house supplies the topic.
- The Lord of the Year's natal condition describes how the year works.
- Transits to the Lord of the Year or through the profected sign become more important.

House-year themes:

- 1st: body, identity, beginnings.
- 2nd: livelihood, money, assets.
- 3rd: siblings, communication, local travel.
- 4th: home, family, land, endings.
- 5th: children, creativity, pleasure.
- 6th: sickness symbolism, labor, subordinates.
- 7th: partners, contracts, open enemies.
- 8th: inheritance, debts, fear, mortality symbolism.
- 9th: long travel, belief, learning, divination.
- 10th: career, rank, authority.
- 11th: allies, patrons, hopes.
- 12th: hidden enemies, confinement, isolation.

## Firdaria

Rules:

- Day charts begin with Sun.
- Night charts begin with Moon.
- Each major chapter has planetary subperiods.
- The major period planet sets the chapter.
- The subperiod planet describes the current paragraph within that chapter.
- Interpret by the planet's natal condition, sect, house, dignity, and relationship to active topics.

Day sequence:

```text
Sun 10, Venus 8, Mercury 13, Moon 9, Saturn 11, Jupiter 12, Mars 7, North Node 3, South Node 2
```

Night sequence:

```text
Moon 9, Saturn 11, Jupiter 12, Mars 7, Sun 10, Venus 8, Mercury 13, North Node 3, South Node 2
```

## Zodiacal Releasing

Rules:

- Starts from a Lot, usually Fortune or Spirit.
- Fortune release emphasizes body and circumstances.
- Spirit release emphasizes career, action, choice, and purpose.
- Periods are nested levels: L1, L2, L3, L4.
- Active sign ruler becomes the period manager.
- Angular signs from Fortune are peak periods: more activity, visibility, and consequence.
- Peak does not mean good; afflicted rulers can make a public crisis.
- Loosing of the Bond is a non-sequential jump to the opposite sign during long periods; it signals disruption, reversal, relocation, career turn, or narrative break.

## Decennials

Rules:

- Select the Apheta through a strict hierarchy.
- Day chart: try sect light Sun first.
- Night chart: try sect light Moon first.
- If rejected, examine the contrary light.
- If lights fail, use the first eligible planet after the Ascendant in zodiacal order.
- Operative houses are prioritized; cadent/inoperative houses weaken eligibility.
- The planetary sequence proceeds by actual zodiacal order from the Apheta, not fixed Chaldean order.
- Period handoff quality depends on the relationship between transmitter and receiver.

Interpretation:

- Same sect, benefic aspect, or reception means smoother transfer.
- Hostile aspect, contrary sect, or malefic dominance means strain.
- Period transitions carry special predictive weight.

## Solar Returns

Rules:

- Solar returns cannot create events absent from natal promise.
- Overlay Solar Return planets onto natal houses.
- Read the Solar Return Ascendant, its ruler, the Moon, the Lord of the Year, and angular planets.
- Return planets in natal houses show where the year presses on the birth chart.

Example rule:

- Solar Return Saturn in natal 2nd emphasizes financial restriction, responsibility, or scarcity symbolism, but only within the natal and profection context.

## Antiscia And Contra-Antiscia

Rules:

- Antiscia mirror points across the solstitial Cancer-Capricorn axis.
- Contra-antiscia mirror across the equinoctial Aries-Libra axis.
- Interpret as hidden conjunctions or hidden oppositions depending on the technique.
- Use as secondary evidence when ordinary aspects do not explain contact.

## Dodecatemoria And Monomoiria

Rules:

- Dodecatemoria projects each sign into micro-zodiacal twelfths.
- Monomoiria assigns degree-level planetary rulership.
- These are refinement tools, especially for rectification and fine-grained character or condition analysis.
- They do not override major dignity, sect, house, or aspect testimony.

## Phasis And Solar Visibility

Rules:

- Phasis tracks whether a planet is emerging from or disappearing into solar light.
- Heliacal rising or emergence increases public visibility and operational force.
- Under beams and combustion reduce visible function.
- Cazimi is an exceptional strengthening state.

## Doryphory

Rules:

- Doryphory identifies spear-bearer or bodyguard planets attending the Sun or Moon.
- A strong attendant planet supports rank, visibility, and executive capacity.
- Judge by sect, dignity, house, and whether the attending relationship is by sign or aspect.

## Decumbiture And Astro-Medical Material

Historical-use-only.

Rules:

- Cast for the moment a patient takes to bed, or historically for first contact with physician if exact onset is unknown.
- Track true lunar motion from the root Moon position.
- Critical points occur at hard lunar arcs, not nominal calendar days.
- Benefic contact at a critical point symbolizes recovery or relief.
- Malefic contact symbolizes worsening or danger.
- Never translate this into medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment direction.

## Mundane And Great Conjunctions

Rules:

- Mundane context can override natal promises in the project philosophy.
- Jupiter-Saturn great conjunction cycles are used for large-scale historical symbolism.
- Elemental mutation is treated as a major era-shift marker.
- Apply mundane indicators as background climate, not individualized certainty.

## Synthesis Rules

Use these rules to resolve contradictions:

- A strong planet in a hard house can act, but the topic is hard.
- A weak planet in a good house may have opportunity but poor execution.
- Benefic dignity without activation is potential, not event.
- Malefic activation with reception can produce hard but productive outcomes.
- Malefic activation without reception tends toward blockage, severing, fear, or damage symbolism.
- Angularity makes testimony louder, not automatically better.
- Sect can soften or sharpen the same planetary condition.
- Time lords decide which natal factors are currently awake.
- The Moon describes flow, timing, and bodily/public responsiveness.
- The Ascendant and its ruler describe the native's agency and condition.
- The 10th and its ruler describe action, rank, career, and public output.
- The 7th and its ruler describe the other party, partners, contracts, and open adversaries.
- The 2nd and 8th are the resource axis: personal resources versus shared obligations.
- The 6th and 12th are adversity houses: labor/illness symbolism versus hidden enemies/isolation.
