# Advanced Mechanics

## Doryphory (Spear-Bearer)
Calculates the bodyguard planets attending the Sun or Moon. Based on Hellenistic definitions of "attending" by sign or aspect.

## Mondomoiria & Dodecatemoria
Micro-zodiacal divisions used for high-precision rectification and character analysis.

## Hermetic Lots
Calculation of the 7 Lots (Fortune, Spirit, Eros, Necessity, Courage, Victory, Nemesis) with sect-based formula reversal.
