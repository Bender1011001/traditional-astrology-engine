# *Ghāyat al-Ḥakīm* (Picatrix), Ritter's critical edition — Arabic

Acquired 2026-08-11. 438 PDF pages. Warburg Institute Digital Collection scan.

## Verified: this is Ritter, not a reprint

The archive.org listing gives only "Picatrix Arabic", 2021, no editor, sitting in a mixed
upload beside a prize-bond leaflet and a pendulum manual. **Rendering a page settles it**:
the Arabic carries Ritter's full critical apparatus with his sigla — `LCVW`, `om. C`,
`Mon.` (the Hebrew witness, in Hebrew type), `Pic.` (the Latin *Picatrix*). That is the
critical edition, not a popular reprint.

Sample content at PDF ~120: **استجلاب قوة القمر في برج الثور** — drawing down the Moon's
power in Taurus — i.e. the astral-magic operations by sign.

## ⚠ Unread, and the pagination is NOT yet established

**No usable text layer.** The only extractable text is the Warburg watermark; the Arabic
does not OCR at all. Every page must be read visually, so knowing the folio↔PDF mapping
matters before any targeted reading.

What is actually known:

- **One clean sample: PDF page 20 carries folio ٢٠ (20)**, suggesting offset 0.
- An earlier pass recorded folios "٢٩٧/٢٩٨" around PDF 120. That is **inconsistent** with
  the above and is most likely a misreading of ١١٩/١٢٠ — Arabic-Indic ٢٩٧ and ١١٩ are not
  hard to confuse at low resolution. **Recorded as a contradiction rather than silently
  dropped**, because the earlier figure is in this session's history and should not be
  trusted by a later reader.
- Automated folio extraction by cropping a fixed fraction of the page height **failed** —
  the text block sits at different heights across the volume, so the crop lands in margin
  on most pages. A proper survey needs full-page renders at intervals, not strip crops.

**So: pagination is provisional (probably offset 0, one sample), and must be confirmed
before citing any Picatrix page number.**
