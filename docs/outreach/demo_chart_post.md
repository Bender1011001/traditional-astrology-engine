# Demo Chart Analysis — Social Proof Content

## PURPOSE

Post a detailed traditional chart analysis using real engine output for a well-known historical
figure. The goal is to show depth and accuracy publicly — not to sell, but to demonstrate that
the engine produces practitioner-grade output worth discussing.

This post should be shared in:
- Reddit (r/Hellenistic_Astrology, r/AstrologyCharts)
- Substack (if you have one, or as a guest post)
- As a standalone page on the site (example-charts section)

**Birth data:** July 13, 100 BCE (Julian), time uncertain (~dawn traditionally used)

easy to verify against biography, Valens discusses similar charts.

Alternative options (if you want certain birth time):
- Marsilio Ficino: October 19, 1433, 1:55 PM LMT, Figline Valdarno, Italy
  (philosopher, physician, astrologer — very appropriate)

  (traditional astrologer himself — practitioners will find this interesting)
- Napoleon Bonaparte: August 15, 1769, 11:30 AM LMT, Ajaccio, Corsica
  (well-documented chart, dramatic life events easy to verify against timing)

**Recommended for first post: William Lilly** — the traditional astrology community will
immediately recognize the name, and it's appropriate to run the engine on the chart of the
man who wrote Christian Astrology.

## WHAT MAKES THIS EFFECTIVE

1. The traditional astrology community has high standards for accuracy — if your output
   is correct, they will say so, and that endorsement is worth more than any ad.

2. William Lilly is a figure everyone in the community knows. Using his chart shows you
   understand the tradition, not just the math.

3. Inviting comparison and correction positions you as confident but not arrogant.
   Real practitioners respect that.

4. The rule ledger excerpt is a differentiator no other tool can show — use it prominently.
